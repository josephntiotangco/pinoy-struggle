Game Updates

November 24, 2026

  • Level 8 · Maypajo. Beat Politiko to unlock three waves with Lubak, Ebike, and Snatcher. Adik makes his Survival debut as the final boss.
  • Level 7 · Malacañang. Beat Justice Delayed to unlock three waves with Matapobre, Stress, and Kahirapan. Politiko closes the level.
  • Level 6 · Padre Faura. Beat Taas Presyo to unlock three waves with Matapobre, Lipunan, and Politiko. Justice Delayed closes the level.
  • Level 5 · Balintawak. Beat Baha to unlock three waves with Ebike, Petrolyo, and Traffic. Taas Presyo closes the level.
  • Monster attacks now have sound cues, from vehicle effects to Taas Presyo's cash register.
  • Almighty Push. Press O or tap Almighty Push with two full Special Bars to knock out every enemy on the field on the spoken cue. The cast empties the meter.

November 23, 2026

  • Level 4 · G. Araneta. Defeat Loans to unlock three new waves with Lubak, Traffic, and Baha. Survival now has 12 waves.
  • Juan deals more damage. Punch deals 30, Kick deals 50, Konoha Senpu deals 80 per enemy hit, and Kamehame Haduken deals 120.
  • Level 3 · Bahay. Face Bills, Stress, and Emotional Damage, then defeat Loans.
  • Level 2 · EDSA. Take on Ebike and Traffic, with Mayabang as the boss.
  • Survival mode. Start in Tondo from Mode Select. Each Charge Token fills one Special Bar.
